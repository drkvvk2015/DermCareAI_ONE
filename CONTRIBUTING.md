# Contributing to DermCareAI

Thank you for contributing to DermCareAI.

## Copyright and contribution provenance

Before submitting a contribution, you must have the right to contribute the material. Do not submit code, documentation, images, models, datasets, credentials, or other material that you do not have permission to redistribute.

Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, contributors retain copyright in their original contributions and grant the project the permissions required by the repository license and the contribution terms accepted with the contribution.

For future commits, contributors should include a Developer Certificate of Origin attestation:

`Signed-off-by: Your Name <your-email@example.com>`

This attestation means the contributor confirms that they have the right to submit the work under the project's contribution terms.

The DCO is a contribution-provenance mechanism; it is **not a copyright assignment**.

## Ownership and relicensing

No contributor should assume that changing the repository's `LICENSE` file transfers or extinguishes another copyright holder's rights. Any future relicensing that requires rights from multiple contributors must be supported by the appropriate written permissions or agreements.

## Review and clinical safety

Changes affecting clinical workflows, AI behavior, patient data, authentication, authorization, billing, pharmacy, auditability, migrations or production infrastructure require review before merge.

AI-related changes must preserve the project's clinical boundary: AI output is decision support and must not silently become a signed diagnosis.
